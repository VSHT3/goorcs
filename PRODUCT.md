# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends playing a private desktop tower-defense game; creators using solo and sandbox play to test rules and balance.

## Product Purpose

Build a readable, playable two-player maze-defense loop before extensive progression. Make content authoring and local verification straightforward.

## Brand Personality

Playful, grounded, restrained. Orcish pixel-art fantasy; concise functional interface copy.

## Anti-references

No marketing-style UI, explanatory filler, decorative stat cards, or labels that describe unimplemented mechanics. BTD Battles informs the competitive loop; BTD6 informs sandbox utility, not copied artwork.

## Design Principles

- Show current facts and available actions; put detailed explanations in tooltips or the Orcieary.
- Preserve the established dark-green pixel UI, clear numeric typography, and fixed gameplay HUD.
- Make keyboard and mouse navigation equally usable.
- Keep presentation local and gameplay outcomes host-authoritative.
- Verify the actual game surface; automated loading alone is not visual proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable text and visible keyboard focus, avoid color-only state indicators, and respect modal/text-input priority over gameplay shortcuts. No formal conformance level has been claimed.
